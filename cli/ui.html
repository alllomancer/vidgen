New avatar from photos

    Order matters: photos and keyframes use the first 3. Best: 1 full-length or waist-up (the main image, also used alone in shots with several avatars), 2 a sharp face close-up, 3 another angle. Use yourself or a fictional character, never a real person without their consent.
